Homemade Spaghetti Bolognese: A Chef’s Guide

Published: Nov 12, 2022 | By Sudip Piya

View Culinary Recipe Box

The Soul of a Great Bolognese

A true Bolognese is more than just meat and tomatoes; it is a slow-cooked labor of love that balances acidity, fat, and umami. Whether you are cooking for a family dinner or serving guests at Munch Time Cafe, the key lies in the patience of the simmer.

Why Buffalo Mince?

In the context of the Nepali market, utilizing high-quality buffalo mince is a cost-effective, flavorful alternative to traditional beef. It holds its texture beautifully under heat and absorbs the aromatic base of garlic and dried chilies perfectly.

Chef’s Tips for Success

  • Aromatic Base: Always sauté your garlic, bay leaves, and dried chilies in olive oil before adding onions to release essential oils.
  • The Sauté: Cook the mince until the liquid completely evaporates; this allows the meat to brown and develop a deeper, richer flavor profile.
  • Balance: If your tomato puree leans too acidic, a small pinch of sugar is the chef's secret to mellowing the sauce.

Storage & Troubleshooting

  • Storage: This sauce is freezer-friendly. Store it in an airtight container for up to three days in the refrigerator or one month in the freezer.
  • Troubleshooting: If the sauce becomes too thick, stir in a splash of warm pasta water just before serving to regain the perfect silky consistency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use noodles instead of spaghetti?

Yes, if spaghetti is unavailable, high-quality local egg noodles provide an excellent texture that clings to the sauce.

Is blue cheese necessary?

It is entirely optional. It adds a unique, creamy depth, but the recipe remains delicious without it.

⏱️ Prep time: 15 mins
🔥 Cook time: 30 mins
🍽️ Servings: 4

Ingredients List

  • • 400g minced meat (beef/lamb/buffalo)
  • • Spaghetti pasta or Egg Noodles
  • • 1 medium diced onion
  • • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • • Homemade tomato puree
  • • Dried red chilies & bay leaves
  • • Butter, black pepper, salt, sugar
  • • Fresh oregano, basil, chives
  • • Optional: Danish blue vein cheese

Method Steps

  1. Sauté garlic, dried chilies, and bay leaves in olive oil.
  2. Add diced onion and cook until translucent.
  3. Add minced meat and cook until liquid evaporates.
  4. Stir in butter, nutmeg, black pepper, and salt.
  5. Pour in tomato puree and simmer until thickened.
  6. Add a bit of blue cheese for richness and sugar to balance acidity.
  7. Boil spaghetti; toss with sauce and a splash of pasta water.
  8. Garnish with fresh herbs and chili flakes.
